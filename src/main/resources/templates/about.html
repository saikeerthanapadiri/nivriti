Learn About Panic Attacks

Understanding what you're experiencing can make it feel a little less overwhelming.

🌊 What is a Panic Attack?

A panic attack is a sudden episode of intense fear or discomfort that causes strong physical and emotional reactions, even when there is no real danger.

❤️ Common Symptoms

• Racing heartbeat
• Shortness of breath
• Sweating
• Dizziness
• Trembling
• Feeling detached or afraid

🌿 What Helps?

Slow breathing, grounding exercises, drinking water, focusing on your surroundings, and speaking to someone you trust can help reduce anxiety.

🚫 Avoid

Don't fight the panic or judge yourself for feeling anxious. Avoid excessive caffeine if it worsens your symptoms.

🤝 Helping Someone Else

Stay calm, reassure them that they are safe, encourage slow breathing, and remain with them until they feel more settled.

☎️ When to Seek Help

If panic attacks happen frequently, interfere with daily life, or cause significant distress, consider reaching out to a mental health professional for support.

🌿 Every step toward understanding is a step toward healing.
